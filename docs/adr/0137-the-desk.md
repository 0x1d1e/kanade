# 137. The Desk: read-only faces of Services on the desktop

Status: accepted. Reverses the non-goal "No desktop widgets" of `docs/archived/design.md` (§ Explicit non-goals), on purpose. Amended by [ADR 0140](0140-desk-notes-edit-in-place.md): the notes card (`desk.notes`) is typed into in place, so the Desk's window takes `Keyboard::OnDemand` while the pointer is on that card or it is being typed into; every other face, and the Desk otherwise, keeps `Keyboard::None`. Amended by [ADR 0141](0141-files-is-a-shelf-not-a-manager.md): the "no file manager" this ADR keeps is kept, and the recent-files item ships as a read-only Surface of the Island (the Files shelf), not as a Desk face and not as a manager. Amended by [ADR 0143](0143-desk-faces-move-by-their-handle.md): a face stands where `desk.place` puts it, else in the corner stack, the window is the whole output and each face is drawn in its own box, and a handle on a face moves it. Builds on [ADR 0006](0006-island-on-top-layer.md) (layers), [ADR 0004](0004-wake-sources-on-announcements.md) (idle cost) and [ADR 0028](0028-the-runtime-is-ported-from-amane.md) (the runtime owns windows). Numbers skip 0045.

## Context

The first design refused desktop widgets, to stay a shell around the Island and not a desktop environment. Kanade is a lab (AGENTS.md): the clock, the next event and the track playing belong on the desktop as much as on the Island, and a user with an empty desktop and a wallpaper they chose wants the time on it without a window open. The Island is the default core, not the only one; where the desktop shows these is not the core's business.

## Decision

- **The non-goal is reversed.** Kanade draws things on the desktop. It still is not a desktop environment: no icons, no file manager, no panels. (The recent files are a read-only Surface of the Island, not a manager, and not a Desk face: [ADR 0141](0141-files-is-a-shelf-not-a-manager.md).)
- **A Desk widget** is a read-only face of an existing Service, drawn as plain text on the desktop in a window of its own, one per monitor. It is not an Activity (no Arbiter, no Lifetime, nothing preempts it), not a Surface, not a Satellite and not a Pane. It reads Services and writes none. In code it is a *Desk* item, never "widget": `crates/runtime/src/widgets/` already means layout primitives.
- **A `desk` Module**, `requires: &[CORE]`, in `src/desk.rs`. It belongs to the shell whatever core is chosen, so it lives outside `island/` and the boundary rules of `src/boundary.rs` are unchanged. It opens one window per monitor (`app.window_per_monitor`), following monitors as they come and go; the runtime closes a gone monitor's window, and the Desk keeps no per-monitor state to forget.
- **Layer.** `Layer::Bottom`, `Zone::Ignore`, `Keyboard::None`, namespace `kanade-desk`, anchored by corner and margin (fixed size, `anchor_*` and `margin`); it reserves nothing. Bottom is under normal windows and above the wallpaper, so the Desk is the desktop a window sits on and a window hides it, as an icon would be.
- **Config.** `desk.clock`, `desk.media`, `desk.weather`, `desk.agenda`, `desk.notes` (each bool, off by default) and `desk.clock_corner` (`top-left`, `top-right`, `bottom-left`, `bottom-right`; default `bottom-right`), all per output. The corner is the Desk's, whichever faces are on: one window per output, the clock, then the weather, the agenda and the track line, then the media buttons. (The corner key keeps the name it was first shipped with.) Each face takes its own `desk.<face>` switch, off by default. Private content has its own switch, also off: `desk.agenda_titles` (the event's title) and `desk.media_titles` (the track's title and artist).
- **The first face is the clock:** the time (`clock::now`, in `clock` 12 or 24 hours) large, and the date line (`clock::today`) under it, white plain text on the wallpaper. No tinted plate: the wallpaper is the Desk's ground, and a plate would make it a window. No new niri layer rule.
- **Private content is off by default.** A face that shows what is private has its own switch, off, apart from the face's own. `desk.agenda_titles` adds the event's title to the agenda line; off, the line is the time and how long until it (`14:30, in 20 min`), never the title, and the title is not logged. `desk.media_titles` draws the track's title and artist; off, neither is drawn and the Desk does not read the player for them. The clock and the weather show none. The buttons are not private.
- **Idle cost.** With `desk` on and every face off the window is `.visible(false)`: it attaches no buffer and reads no Service (CONTEXT.md: a Module that is off starts no thread, opens no window and reads no Service). With the clock on, the only idle frame is the clock's one a minute, armed by `clock::now` and nothing else (ADR 0004). Weather and the agenda add no poll. Weather redraws when the weather Service is written. The agenda, while its switch and the `calendar` Module are on, uses that same minute tick so the next event can appear and leave; it does not start a timer of its own. The track line redraws when the player caption changes, and only while `desk.media_titles` is on. With the Module off, nothing exists.
- **No lock-screen presence.** The Desk is not drawn on the lock screen (`crates/lock`, ADR 0025): the lock holds the session, and its own surface covers the outputs. The Desk is a layer under windows; the session lock covers it.
- **Hidden while niri's overview is open.** The window is `.visible(false)` then, built before any face, so a hidden Desk reads no clock and takes no tick. It follows the overview through a small `Overview` Service written from `Niri` only when the flag changes, so the Desk does not redraw at each of niri's events; it reads `Niri`, not `IslandService`, as it belongs to no core. The `desk` Module needs niri (`Provider::Niri`): without it the overview cannot be known and the Desk would be shown in it. Nothing is placed in the overview's backdrop.

## Why not another place

- **`Layer::Background`**: niri draws the wallpaper tool's surface (awww) there, and a Desk window on it would sit beside the wallpaper with no defined order against it. Bottom is above Background, so the wallpaper is always under the Desk.
- **A normal window**: niri would tile it, give it focus and a place in the Dock and the overview, and the user would have to float and pin it with window rules. A Desk is not a window the user manages.
- **A Surface**: a Surface is the Island's, opened by a user action and drawn on the Island's body (CONTEXT.md); the Desk is on while the user does nothing and stays put when the Island is another core.

## Interaction

The clock is non-interactive: `.click_through()` gives the window an empty input region. The second slice adds the media buttons, below, which are the only part that takes the pointer. The constraints:

- **Bottom layer kept.** An interactive face takes the pointer only inside an input region of its drawn controls (`LayerWindow::input_region`, built from `view::input_area` as `dock.rs` and `view/mod.rs` do). The text around the controls stays click-through. With niri's overview open, or the Module off, the region is empty and the window hidden, as `view/mod.rs` empties the Island's region under the overview.
- **`Keyboard::None`, always.** No Desk control is focused by a key on the layer itself. `Keyboard::OnDemand` on a Bottom layer would drop the user's toplevel focus (`island/service.rs`, the focus handling), and niri's behaviour there is untested. **Amended by ADR 0140**, for the notes card only: it takes `Keyboard::OnDemand` while the pointer is on it or it is being typed into, since a surface is typed into only once it is focused, and every other face keeps `Keyboard::None`.
- **An effect is a Kanade verb** behind its Module gate, reached by key from the Launcher's commands ([ADR 0112](0112-launcher-runs-a-closed-set-of-kanade-verbs.md)), a niri keybind, and the matching Surface whose ring holds the same targets. This is a documented exception to CONTEXT.md's "every target in a Surface is a key away": that covers Surfaces, and Desk items are not Surfaces. A keyboard story for the Desk itself (a ring on the layer) is deferred to a follow-up ADR with its own E2E.
- **First interactive face, built (second slice, `desk.media`):** Media transport, previous / play-pause / next, through the same `media::request(Transport, ..)` as `kanade media`, posted to the media follower with a detached `Later`, so the view makes no bus call and writes no Service. The `media` Module is optional to `desk`: with it off nobody follows players and no button shows. What the buttons may ask is the `Remote` Service (`sources/playback.rs`), written by `media::follow` at all times and only when it changes (the player `kanade media` would ask: its play state, `CanPlay`/`CanPause`, `CanGoPrevious`, `CanGoNext`); `Playback` is written only while the Media Surface is open and could not serve. With no player open or the overview open, the input region is empty and the buttons are not drawn. A button the player cannot act on is drawn faded, is left out of the input region and is a disabled `Button` to `kanade ui`. The input region is the three circles only (`view::input_area`); a click beside them goes to the window below. `kanade ui` names them `media/previous|play-pause|next` in the window `desk` (`--window desk`). The buttons and the play state are not private. Title and artist are a later face, `desk.media_titles`, not part of the button row: a click on that text still passes through.
- **Text faces (third slice).** `desk.weather` draws the temperature and condition from the weather Service already fetched; with the `weather` Module off, or no forecast, it draws nothing and does not read Weather. `desk.agenda` draws the next timed event from `calendar::upcoming`, the path Rest uses, and needs `calendar` on. `desk.agenda_titles` (off) is the only way the title is drawn; off, the line is the time and how far off, and the title is not logged. `desk.media_titles` (off) draws the title and artist from a caption the media follower writes when the player changes; the Desk reads it only while that switch is on. None of these take the pointer. The window grows by a line for each that has something to say; the button region stays the three circles at the bottom of that window.
- A later face with a text field or a ring needs its own ADR and an E2E of niri's focus on a Bottom layer. The first such face is the notes card: [ADR 0140](0140-desk-notes-edit-in-place.md).

## Consequences

- A desktop with the clock on shows it under every window, on every output, with no focus taken and no click taken.
- Text on a wallpaper is white and has no plate, so its legibility depends on the wallpaper. A light wallpaper may make it hard to read; the corner is the user's remedy. Whether a shadow is wanted is decided by looking at it running.
- The runtime needed nothing new: corner anchoring with margin and a fixed size, `Zone::Ignore`, `Layer::Bottom` and input regions all exist. Not verified live: that niri places a Bottom window with `Zone::Ignore` by corner and margin exactly so, and that it delivers pointer clicks to a Bottom layer's input region at all.
- Settings shows the Desk keys in the Shell page's Desk section; the wiki's Configuration page lists them.
