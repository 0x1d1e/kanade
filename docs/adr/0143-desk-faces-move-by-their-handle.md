# 143. Desk faces move by their handle, and stay where they are let go

Status: accepted. Builds on [ADR 0137](0137-the-desk.md) (the Desk) and [ADR 0140](0140-desk-notes-edit-in-place.md) (the notes card). Takes the live-edit grid of "Desktop widgets" in `TODO.md`, one face at a time, without the rest of that item: no grid to snap to, no new kinds of face.

## Context

A Desk's faces stand in one stack from `desk.clock_corner`, and the corner is the only say the user has in where they go. A clock the user wants above a window's left side, or the notes beside the media buttons rather than under them, cannot be had: the corner moves the whole Desk, and nothing moves one face.

Every other desktop that draws a clock on it lets the clock be dragged, and the eye reads a face that cannot be moved as a picture. The Desk draws faces of the shell's own Services, so it is the one place in Kanade where a pointer can place a piece of the shell directly, with no Settings row to find first.

The window was the stack's own size, `desk`'s width by the height of the faces that show, anchored by its corner: one box per output, so a face could not stand apart from the others.

## Decision

- **A place per face, per output.** `desk.place` is a table of `face = { x = 100, y = 200 }`, its top left corner in whole logical pixels from the output's own, in an output's table as the Desk's other keys are (`[output."eDP-1".desk.place]`). A face given none stands in the corner stack, exactly as it does today, so an existing config draws the Desk unchanged and a face turned off and on again keeps its place. The faces it may name are the five the Desk draws: `clock`, `weather`, `agenda`, `notes`, `media`.
- **The window is the output.** A face that is not in the stack can stand anywhere, so the Desk's window is the whole output (`WindowSize::Full`, no margin, still `Layer::Bottom`, `Zone::Ignore`, anchored by corner for the hiding and nothing else) and each face is drawn in its own box laid out inside it. What the pointer may take stays an input region of the drawn controls only, so the output-sized window takes no more click than before.
- **The stack is the same stack.** With no place from the config, the faces stand where they always have: the corner's margin from the edges, one under the other in the order the Desk draws them (clock, weather, agenda, notes, media), each box `desk`'s width. The title line and the buttons of the player share one box, so the track's title stays over its buttons once either is moved. Where both `desk.media_titles` and `desk.notes` are on, that box stands after the card, so the track's line sits under the card rather than over it; that is the one place the default look changes.
- **The handle.** Each face carries a small faded bar at its inner edge, in the face's own box, on the side away from the corner (the card and the track's line draw from the outer edge, so the bar covers no text). A press on the bar takes hold of the face's box; every move after it carries the box by the same distance the pointer moved, and the release lets it go. A press on the face anywhere else is what it was: nothing on a text face, a click into the card on the notes, a button on the row. So typing into the notes loses nothing, and a modifier is not needed: the bar is the one part of a face that moves it.
- **Kept on the output, off the Island.** A face is drawn only where all of it lies on its output, a `place::MARGIN` (48 px) from each edge, and that clamp is also what a place the config gives is drawn at, so a place that no longer fits (another output, a smaller mode) is moved in rather than lost. The Desk does not know where the Island is, and reads no core to find out; a face may stand under the Island's strip where the Island is another core or no Island is drawn. Not overlapping the Island therefore rests on the corner's own margin and on the user's hand, and is the one part of this slice that is a rule of thumb rather than an invariant.
- **The write is off the draw thread, once, and only on the release.** A release whose box did not move writes nothing. A release that moved it posts the place through the Settings file's own write path (`settings::set`, the path `kanade module` takes) on a worker (`std::thread::spawn`, joined by nothing), so the draw thread waits on no disk and the place lands in `settings.toml`'s own output table, where a reload draws it like any other edit. Until then the drag's own box is drawn, so the face does not jump back and forth across the reload. A write the file refuses puts the face back where it stood and says why once, on stderr, as the Desk's other refusals are said.
- **Escape, and the keyboard leaving, put the face back.** The window holds `Keyboard::OnDemand` while the pointer is over a bar, before the press that would otherwise focus nothing, and while a hand is on one, exactly as the notes card arms itself on hover (ADR 0140). Escape drops the drag and draws the face where it stood, and so does the keyboard going elsewhere: a drag whose Escape can no longer arrive is not one to keep. Nothing else of the Desk takes a key: with the pointer off the bar the keyboard goes as it came, and the notes card's own keys and caret are unchanged. A keyboard that leaves the window mid-drag is an end of the drag as well.
- **A drag ends with the Desk.** The overview opening, or the face's switch going off, hides the window, and the drag goes with it: the window is built hidden before any face, and the drag's state is cleared. Nothing is kept where the pointer left it when the face it was moving is no longer drawn.
- **`kanade ui` does not name the bar.** A bar acts on a hold, and a `press` is a tap that does nothing, as the Clipboard's Clear does; the bar stays out of the tree rather than being described as something a script cannot press.

## Consequences

- The Desk's window is output-sized, which the compositor holds for the whole output rather than a box: the input region still keeps every click outside the controls going through, but the surface is larger to composite than before.
- With `desk.media_titles` and `desk.notes` both on, the track's line moves under the card the first time this ships, and stays wherever it is dragged.
- A place is per output: the same face on two monitors is dragged twice, which the output's own table makes explicit, and a monitor that goes away keeps its place in the file.
- A place that a smaller mode or another monitor no longer fits is clamped for drawing but not rewritten, so plugging the monitor back in restores the place it had.
- The Desk still cannot be placed above normal windows, in the overview, or on the lock screen; nor can a face be resized, snapped to a grid, or stacked over another. Each would be its own decision.
- **Not run live**: that a press on a bar is delivered to a Bottom layer's input region and that a drag follows the pointer (unit-tested: the stack, the clamp, the drag's offset, the config's round trip and its refusal, Escape's restore; E2E NOT RUN), and how the bar reads on a wallpaper.
